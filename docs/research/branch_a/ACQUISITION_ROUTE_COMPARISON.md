## A next check must establish the exact route rather than the vendor label

### R01 · Owned adapter over entitled official organization APIs

Resolve intended use and exact granted tier without transmitting credentials; then one eligible organization publication and bounded continuation.

**Failure rule:** Expired grant/role loss stops eligible access. A tier-disabled operation must not be attempted as a parser workaround.

### R02 · Owned organizational structured web-client reader

T14 one permitted organizational publication, with identity/root/time and optional one continuation; retain actual source status without endpoint sweeps.

**Failure rule:** Source challenge or restriction stops use; no alternative account/proxy as evasion. Typed partial run must retain previously committed pages.

### R03 · Owned public organizational page reader

T10 minimal permitted raw-response/DOM comparison; T11 known organization original/repost correspondence; no source-safe scrolling threshold assumed.

**Failure rule:** Login/challenge, selector miss and confirmed empty window remain separate. Retaining old feed data must not advertise new source freshness.

### R04 · Unipile company-publication reader

Obtain minimized organizational response schema and field-origin definitions under appropriate entitlement; reconcile provider IDs/social IDs and account failures.

**Failure rule:** Disconnected/credential/permission/source-restriction states must stay failures, not empty results. An upstream account challenge is not made independent by using a provider.

### R05 · Bright Data organizational post extraction/discovery

Verify exact organizational endpoint/body/schema and compliant stop behavior, then examine one minimized result and its job/error receipt; do not implement tutorial assumptions blindly.

**Failure rule:** Persist pending snapshot_id and reconcile existing job before resubmitting; inspect per-input errors; failed is terminal unless an explicitly safe retry policy applies.

### R06 · Coresignal Company Posts dataset/API

Obtain Company Posts—not Employee Posts or company-profile—dictionary and publication-only sample with native URL/ID, source/crawl/update clocks and dataset version.

**Failure rule:** Dataset API/delivery outages and snapshot age remain separate from LinkedIn source availability; removals need documented reasons.

### R07 · Coresignal Multi-Source Company records

Request organization-only dictionary/time semantics and prove any linkage by source-native identity; do not treat enriched/inferred fields as LinkedIn observations.

**Failure rule:** Fresh API response can contain older observations; schema and removed upstream sources need versioned handling.

### R08 · Admin-provided exports and independently supplied organizational publications

Inspect one minimized organization export or authorized first-party publication; preserve source label and supplied provenance without impersonating a direct LinkedIn capture.

**Failure rule:** Missing file/time/range/provenance blocks claims of coverage; imports never silently establish recurring source monitoring.
